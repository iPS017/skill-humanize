## 1. Punctuation and characters

| Tell | Fix |
|---|---|
| Em dash `—` | Comma, period, parentheses or a colon, depending on what the sentence needs. |
| Spaced en dash ` – ` used as an em dash | Same. En dash stays fine in ranges: "2020–2024". |
| Space before a colon `word :` | No space in English: `word:`. Same for `;` `!` `?`. |
| Dramatic colon ("The result: …", "The catch?") | Write a plain sentence. |
| Lines of "**Label**: explanation" | Plain sentences, or a simple list without bold labels. |
| Ellipsis character `…` | Three dots, or rewrite. |
| Arrows `→` `⇒` | "so", "then", or nothing. |
| Decorative emojis | Remove. |
| Curly quotes in technical text, code, configs | Straight quotes. |

## 2. Words to avoid

None of these are wrong. They are overused by models, and a cluster of them reads as generated.

**Verbs**: delve, dive into, embark, navigate (figurative), unlock, unleash, harness, leverage, elevate, empower, foster, bolster, streamline, revolutionize, transform, showcase, underscore, highlight (as filler), resonate, spearhead.

**Adjectives**: seamless, robust, powerful, cutting-edge, game-changing, groundbreaking, innovative, vibrant, bustling, comprehensive, meticulous, intricate, pivotal, crucial, essential, vital, paramount, holistic, dynamic, nuanced, invaluable.

**Nouns**: tapestry, testament, landscape, realm, journey, beacon, symphony, synergy, ecosystem (outside tech), cornerstone, game-changer, deep dive.

**Transitions in a row**: moreover, furthermore, additionally, in addition, consequently, notably, importantly, ultimately.

**Openers**: "In today's fast-paced world", "In the ever-evolving landscape of", "Imagine a world where", "Have you ever wondered", "Whether you're a X or a Y", "Let's dive in", "In this article, we'll explore".

**Filler**: "It's worth noting that", "It's important to remember", "It goes without saying", "At the end of the day", "When it comes to", "In terms of", "Needless to say".

**Closers**: "In conclusion", "In summary", "Overall", "Feel free to reach out", "I hope this helps", "Happy coding!".

**Chatbot voice**: "Great question!", "Certainly!", "Absolutely!", "Here's the thing", "Here's a breakdown", "Let me know if you'd like", "I'd be happy to".

## 3. Patterns and structures

- "It's not just X, it's Y" / "not only… but also" used as a reveal. Say Y.
- Groups of three, every time.
- Rhetorical question, then the answer.
- Punchline sentence closing every paragraph ("And that changes everything.").
- Balanced both-sides framing when one option is clearly better.
- Stacked hedges: "may potentially", "could arguably", "to some extent".
- A conclusion that restates the intro.
- Every sentence 15 to 25 words long.

## 4. Formatting

- No headings in short answers or short posts.
- Bold rarely.
- Bullets only for parallel items, paragraphs for reasoning.
- No TL;DR box on a short text.

## 5. Before/after examples

Before:

After:
> I build portfolio and marketing sites with Next.js and GSAP. I handle design handoff, animation and deployment. My last client site loads in under a second on a mid-range phone.

Before (README):
> ## ✨ Features
> - 🚀 **Blazing fast**: Built with cutting-edge technologies
> - 🎨 **Beautiful UI**: A seamless and intuitive experience

After:
> ## Features
> - Static export, around 80 kB of JS on the home page
> - Light and dark themes that follow the system setting
